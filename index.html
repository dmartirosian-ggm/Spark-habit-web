How Spark works

Set it up in two minutes. Then it's one tap per habit.

Pick a few habits

Add your child's name and age, and Spark suggests routines that fit, from "Use the potty" for toddlers to "Pack backpack" for preschoolers. Choose the days and the time of day for each.

Check them off

When a habit is done, tap it. Every check-off is a star and a little celebration. A gentle reminder can nudge you at morning, afternoon, or bedtime.

Show your child

Turn the phone around. The full-screen Show view gives your child big pictures of what they did today and how many stars they need for their reward.

Questions

Who is Spark for?

Spark is for parents and caregivers of children ages 2 to 6. The grown-up uses the app; your child doesn't need their own account or device. The Show view is the part made for your child to look at.

What do I need to sign up?

You can sign in with Apple, Google, or your email address. An account lets your data stay in sync across devices and with other grown-ups you invite.

Does Spark have ads or tracking?

No. Spark has no ads and no third-party analytics or advertising SDKs, and we never sell your data. Read the details in our Privacy Policy.

Can more than one grown-up use it?

Yes, with Spark Plus. Invite a partner, grandparent, or sitter with a code. Everyone in the family sees the same children, habits, and stars, and one Plus subscription covers the whole household.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Open the Settings app on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose Spark. You can cancel anytime, and Plus stays active until the end of the period you paid for.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, go to Family and tap Delete account. This permanently deletes your account. If you're the only grown-up in your family, your children, habits, stars, rewards, and photos are deleted too.
