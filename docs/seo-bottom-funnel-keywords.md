# Spark: bottom-of-funnel keywords

US Google, DataForSEO (Google Ads volume and Labs keyword difficulty), pulled Oct 18, 2026. KD is 0–100; lower is easier. "–" means DataForSEO had no score.

This adds to the main SEO plan (chore charts, reward charts, potty training and routines hubs). It covers searches from people looking for **a product**, not advice: "X app", "best X", "X review", "X vs Y". Following the playbook, product searches get **landing pages**, not blog posts.

The homepage already targets **chore chart app** (390, KD 13).

Why these are winnable:
- **potty training app:** the first page of results is weak. It's two app store listings for one competitor (Potty Whiz), a 2016 Romper article, a Reddit thread about puppies, and Pull-Ups and Go Potty brand pages. A focused page can get onto page one. KD is 2.
- **family chore app / chore app:** the results are mostly comparison articles (PointUp, Homsy, Twigby) and BusyKid. Many people searching this have older kids, so lead with the angle no competitor owns: **built for ages 2–6, with pictures instead of words**.
- **visual schedule app:** much of this traffic comes from parents of autistic kids (Choiceworks ranks here). Spark's picture routines fit, but don't claim it's a therapy or special-needs tool.

Keep these pages accurate to the app. Spark has no potty **timer**, so don't target "potty training timer app" (170) unless one gets built.

Pages for searches like "goally alternative" have almost no traffic. Mention each competitor in the "best chore apps" post instead of giving each a page. The one exception worth testing is a **Joon app review** page (110/mo, and the high ad price means advertisers find it valuable). Joon is aimed at ages 6–12 and ADHD, so the honest angle is "for younger kids, use Spark".

Greenlight and BusyKid are allowance and debit-card apps for older kids, so they aren't a fit.

## Skip

- **star chart app** (1,300): astronomy, not reward charts.
- **chore app for teens / adults** (170 each): wrong age group.
- **greenlight chores**, **busykid**: allowance and banking intent.

## Suggested order

1. `/potty-training-app/`: easiest keyword (KD 2), weakest competition, and it links to the potty hub.
2. `/family-chore-app/`: highest combined volume (chore app 1,300 + family chore app 720 + free chore app 320).
3. "Best Chore Apps for Families (2026)" comparison post.
4. `/digital-chore-chart/` and `/visual-schedule-app/`.
5. Joon app review (test).
